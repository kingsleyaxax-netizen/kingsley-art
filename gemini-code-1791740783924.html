Biography

About Kingsley Ong An Xuan

Kingsley Ong An Xuan is a fine artist and illustrator based in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia. Specializing in traditional mediums including fine colored pencil rendering, graphite drafting, and illustrative studies, his work explores dynamic perspective, anatomical flow, and high-precision detail.

Over a twelve-year period of structured artistic training, Kingsley developed a strong foundation in foundational draftsmanship before advancing into refined stylistic expression and character studies.
